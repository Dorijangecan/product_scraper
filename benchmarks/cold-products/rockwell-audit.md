## Reproduction and repairs

The initial individual runs reproduced three failures: `440R-S23174` was partial because the generic Safety Relay profile required an unpublished current field; `800T-A2` was partial because a passive contact operator was treated as requiring supply voltage; and `140G-N0I3-E12-Z1` was classified as generic Circuit Breaker. The minimal fixes were Rockwell-specific quality exceptions for 440R and 800T in `src/shared/product-requirements.ts`, plus a Rockwell `140G` family mapping to `Molded Case Circuit Breaker` in `src/server/scrapers/device-type-families.ts`. All ten items were rerun individually after the fixes and passed.

Validation: `npx tsc --noEmit` and the full Vitest suite passed (119 files, 2327 tests). Individual final benchmark reports are the ten `benchmark-report.manufacturer-rockwell.catalogNumber-*.json` files beside this audit.
