## Reproduction and repair

Before the fix, the live runs classified `TBEN-S2-4IOL` as `Programmable Logic Controller`, left `TBEN-L5-PLC-10` as `unknown`, and reported the industrial switch as generic `Switch`. The official family names are authoritative: TBEN-S2 is an I/O module, TBEN-L5-PLC is a compact PLC, and TBEN-L4-SE is an industrial switch. The smallest safe change was a Turck-only family map in `src/server/scrapers/device-type-families.ts`: PLC prefixes first, then TBEN/FEN I/O prefixes, then TBEN-L4/L5-SE switch prefixes. All ten items were rerun individually after that change and passed.

Validation: `npx tsc --noEmit` passed; the ten post-fix benchmark reports are the individual `benchmark-report.manufacturer-turck.catalogNumber-*.json` files beside this audit.
