Chillers Blue e 8 - 48 kW

Article no.: SK 3336460

The Blue e chiller in the floor standing enclosure provides centralised, efficient cooling of the cooling water. The microchannel technology reduces the volume of refrigerant required. Intuitive operation via touch display and intelligent communications interfaces ensure user-friendly operation and analysis. Integral safety functions, included as standard, ensure maximum safety. Delivery times are also shortened, thanks to pre-defined option packages.
