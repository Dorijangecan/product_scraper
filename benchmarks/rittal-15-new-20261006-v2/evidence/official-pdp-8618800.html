Section for cable entry, centre

Article no.: VX 8618800

The section is fitted directly onto the gland plates. In this way, when installing the mounting plate in the forward mounting position, the cables may be routed directly onto a cable clamp rail without large bending radii. In conjunction with the cable clamp rail, this enables optimum adjustment between the cable entry and mounting plate.
