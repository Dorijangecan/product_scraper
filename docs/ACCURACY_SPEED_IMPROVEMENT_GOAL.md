# Accuracy and speed improvement goal

## Objective

Improve `product_scraper` one manufacturer at a time until it can identify and scrape a broad range of device types accurately and process each device in about 2.5 seconds. Accuracy is a prerequisite for speed: do not optimize a batch until every product in it passes the evidence and field checks below.

## Manufacturer and batch sequence

1. Work on one manufacturer at a time.
2. Select five new, previously unprocessed products that represent different device types or meaningful subtypes. Check benchmark fixtures, reports, aliases, database history, and cached product URLs before selecting them.
3. Run a cold, isolated scrape of all five.
4. Review every product independently. Confirm exact catalog identity, the manufacturer's own product page, device type, important type-specific values and their source, relevant documents, and the actual product image when available.
5. Fix shared extraction, classification, normalization, or source-selection causes. Keep unsupported or ambiguous values blank or marked for review; never fill them from a sibling product.
6. Rerun the same five cold and isolated. Repeat review and repair until each product passes the accuracy criteria.
7. Only after accuracy passes, optimize the acquisition path toward approximately 2.5 seconds per device. Rerun all five and confirm that the accuracy checks still pass.
8. Move to the next five new products from that manufacturer only after the batch is accurate and meets the speed goal. Complete about three or four batches, covering roughly 15–20 products, before moving to the next manufacturer.

## Acceptance criteria per product

- Exact product identity is supported by the official page or an exact-product document; a family match alone is insufficient.
- Device type matches the product's function.
- Important fields for the device type match official source evidence. Electrical values preserve their context and rating conditions; independent ratings are not combined into a fabricated operating point.
- The official product URL is exact, and the expected documents are available. Images are visually checked for the exact product rather than accepted solely because a download succeeded.
- Uncertain or unavailable values remain blank or review-required.
- PDT audit has no unproven written values. Enum mismatches are left blank and recorded rather than forced into a nearby option.
- Accuracy is rechecked after the speed change.

## Speed measurement

Use a cold isolated run with the same benchmark setup before and after changes. Report per-device acquisition time, mean, median, maximum, and the complete benchmark time separately. The 2.5-second target applies to device processing; report export and document handling separately so they remain visible. Never improve the headline time by hiding checks or dropping a required source, field, image, or document.

## Records

Keep a manifest and a review README for every batch. Record novelty checks, official product links, important source facts, corrections, image review, test and type-check results, benchmark report path, acceptance status, enum skips, unproven writes, and comparable before/after timings. Mark a batch complete only when all five products pass accuracy and speed.
