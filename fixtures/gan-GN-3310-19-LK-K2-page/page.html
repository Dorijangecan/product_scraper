GN 3310 Switches / Indicator Lights Stainless Steel, with / without LED Lightning

Contact type: LK - Indicator light LED, red / green (bi-color) (no switching function)
Connection type: K2 - Cable, end open, 2 m

Product Family Stainless Steel Quality Class 2

The Ganter product range includes a large number of standard parts made of high-quality, non-rusting stainless steels. Thanks to their outstanding material properties, these are used in a wide array of industries. Their corrosion and rust resistance in particular contributes to the value stability and longevity of machines and equipment.

Product description

Depending on the type, buttons / indicator lights GN 3310 can send electrical control signals and indicate the status of a machine control, for example, via the integrated LEDs.

Depending on the connection type, they are connected via a cable with an open end or via a plug.

The LEDs light up red or green depending on the polarity at the connections.
