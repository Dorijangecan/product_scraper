Product description

Cabinet U-handles GN 6284 have a button and RGB LED lighting on the back of the handle.

Depending on the identification no., the button is fitted with a break or make contact or two break contacts and triggers an electrical control signal, such as for a machine control.

The integrated RGB LED handle lighting can serve as a status indicator for a controlle, for example. Various colors can be realized by combining red, blue and green light. The combinations can be found in the table.

Complementary cabinet U-handles without switching function are
available as GN 6284.1.
